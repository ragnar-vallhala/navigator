# Terrain textures

These images are the ground photo textures of the simulator terrain. They are
Poly Haven assets released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), so they
may be used, modified and redistributed without conditions and without
attribution. The credit to the scanning artists is given anyway on each asset
page.

| `rocky_trail_02_diff_1k.jpg`, `rocky_trail_02_nor_gl_1k.jpg` | <https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail_02> |
| `brown_mud_03_diff_1k.jpg`, `brown_mud_03_nor_gl_1k.jpg` | <https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_03> |

`_diff` is the diffuse (albedo) map and `_nor_gl` the OpenGL-convention normal
map. They are the 1K (1024 x 1024) JPEG downloads, re-encoded with metadata
removed at JPEG quality 85 (diffuse) and 80 (normal) to keep the application
small; the content is otherwise unchanged.
